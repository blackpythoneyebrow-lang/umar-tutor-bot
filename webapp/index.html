Grammatika Qo'llanmasi B1

Present Simple (Oddiy Hozirgi Zamon)

I/You/We/They + V1 | He/She/It + V1 + s/es

Muntazam, har kuni takrorlanadigan odatiy ish-harakatlar va faktlar uchun qo'llaniladi.

❌ Ko'p uchraydigan XATO:
She go to school every day.
✅ TO'G'RI shakli:
She goes to school every day.
💡 Nega xato? (Chuqur tushuntirish):

Uchinchi shaxs birlikda (He, She, It) fe'llarga doimo -s yoki -es qo'shimchasi qo'shiladi: go -> goes, play -> plays.

Misollar:
  • I drink tea every morning.
  • He watches English movies on Fridays.
